Case Study 01

From a Silent List to an Engaged Audience

Client: Mrs. Bibi George  |  Niche: Communication (Public Speaking & Diction)

The Problem

Mrs. Bibi had an email list on Mailchimp — but it had been silent for a long time. No consistent emails were going out, no relationship was being built, and her subscribers had essentially gone cold. The list existed, but it was not working for her.

The Solution

I came in with a clear plan. First, I crafted a re-engagement sequence designed to wake up her cold Mailchimp subscribers and move the active ones over to her new ESP, Systeme.io. A few of them made the move. I also wrote a message she could send to her past buyers, inviting them to join her new list on Systeme.io.

From there, I built a welcome sequence to re-introduce her to those who already knew her, and to properly introduce her to those who were just discovering her for the first time.

Finally, we established a consistent sending rhythm — one intentional email every Friday — so her list could start to know, like, and trust her.

The Process

I began with the re-engagement and migration strategy to salvage and transfer her existing audience. Once the new list was set up on Systeme.io, I wrote the welcome sequence to set the right tone from day one.

From there, I took over her weekly emails, crafting one email every Friday, each one designed to engage, educate, and deepen the relationship between Mrs. Bibi and her audience. Emails go out every Friday as a consistent weekly touchpoint. There were occasions where unforeseen circumstances meant an email went out on a different day, but consistency was always the priority and any deviation from the Friday schedule was the exception, not the rule.

The Results

Since we started working together, Mrs. Bibi's email list has maintained an average open rate of 25.5%, with a 0.0% spam rate — meaning her emails are landing in inboxes and her subscribers are actually reading them. She has also been receiving direct replies from her audience, a strong signal that the emails are resonating on a personal level.

No offer has been launched to the list yet. What you see here is purely relationship building — and the numbers already reflect an engaged, responsive audience ready for when we do.

Case Study 02

How an Abandoned Cart Sequence Recovered Over ₦400,000 in Sales

Client: Oleander Yuba  |  Niche: Communication (Public Speaking & Diction)  |  Service: Abandoned Cart Sequence

The Problem

Mrs. Oleander was running paid ads to sell her low ticket offer, TPS (The Polished Speaker), priced at ₦9,999. The ads were bringing in traffic and people were showing interest, but a number of potential buyers were dropping off at checkout without completing their payment. That money was sitting on the table, unclaimed.

The Solution

I came in and crafted a 3-part abandoned cart email sequence specifically designed to bring those potential buyers back. Each email addressed a different stage of hesitation, acknowledging their concerns, removing barriers to payment, and creating urgency to complete their purchase.

The Process

I studied the offer, understood the audience, and wrote a sequence that spoke directly to the hesitation of someone who had shown interest but not yet committed. Each email in the sequence was intentional, moving the reader closer to a decision without being pushy or desperate.

The Results

The abandoned cart sequence performed exceptionally well, achieving a conversion rate of over 10% and recovering over ₦400,000 in sales that would have otherwise been lost.

Please note that the dashboard data capturing these results is no longer available, as this campaign was run some time ago. However, the results were tracked and recorded during the campaign period.

Case Study 03

How a Strategic Email Campaign Generated ₦400,000 in High Ticket Sales in 30 Days

Client: Oleander Yuba  |  Niche: Communication (Public Speaking & Diction)  |  Service: High Ticket Email Campaign  |  Product: Journey to Eloquence (JTE) — ₦200,000

The Problem

Mrs. Oleander had a high ticket program, Journey to Eloquence, sitting in her offer suite with an existing email list that had not been strategically pitched to it. The challenge with high ticket offers is that they require a different approach entirely. You cannot simply send a sales email and expect results. The audience needed to be qualified, nurtured, and guided through a deliberate process before being presented with the offer.

The Solution

I designed and executed a multi-step email campaign that went beyond a simple promotion. The strategy was built around qualification, ensuring that only the right people were being moved towards the offer, while others were redirected to more suitable products.

The Process

Promotional emails were sent to the list, designed to pique interest and drive clicks. Once a subscriber clicked, they were taken to a page with a series of qualifying questions. Based on their responses, two things could happen:

Those who qualified were offered the opportunity to book a call directly with Mrs. Oleander, who would then close them on Journey to Eloquence.

Those who did not qualify automatically received a follow-up email redirecting them to other products in her offer suite, ensuring no lead was wasted. This was a full system, not just emails, but a strategic pipeline built to filter, qualify, and convert at the right level for each subscriber.

The Results

Within 30 days, the campaign generated 2 sales of Journey to Eloquence, bringing in ₦400,000 in high ticket revenue. It is worth noting that the campaign was not run consistently over an extended period. Given the strategy in place, a longer and more consistent execution would have produced significantly higher results.

Case Study 04

Consistent Nurturing That Keeps an Audience Engaged Week After Week

Client: Oleander Yuba  |  Niche: Communication (Public Speaking & Diction)  |  Service: Weekly Nurturing Emails  |  Duration: May 2025 till date

The Problem

Having an email list is one thing. Keeping that list warm, engaged, and looking forward to your emails every week is another. For Mrs. Oleander, the goal was not just to send emails, but to build a consistent presence in her subscribers' inboxes that educated, added value, and deepened the relationship between her and her audience week after week.

The Solution

I took over the writing of Mrs. Oleander's weekly nurturing emails, crafting one intentional email every Thursday. Each email was written to educate her subscribers on communication, public speaking, and diction, positioning her as a trusted authority in her niche while keeping her audience consistently engaged.

The Process

Every Thursday, a nurturing email goes out to Mrs. Oleander's list. Each email is built around a single idea, concept, or insight relevant to her audience, written in a way that is clear, engaging, and easy to apply. In situations where unforeseen circumstances made it impossible to send on a Thursday, the email was sent on the nearest available day. Consistency was always the priority.

The Results

Below are the open rates for three of the nurturing emails written for this campaign:

•  Email 1 — "Intelligence Doesn't Make You Sound Confident" — 35.79% open rate

•  Email 2 — "Before You Speak, Take Note Of These..." — 29.86% open rate

•  Email 3 — "The Vocabulary Habit That Changes How People Perceive You" — 29.69% open rate

These numbers are consistently above the industry average open rate of 20 to 25%, reflecting an audience that genuinely looks forward to receiving these emails every week.
